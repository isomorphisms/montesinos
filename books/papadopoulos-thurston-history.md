# Papadopoulos on Thurston and the hyperbolic background

This is a selective cross-list from Athanase Papadopoulos's historical work. The criterion here is **actual contact with hyperbolic geometry, geometric structures, Thurston, tessellations/orbifolds, or the conceptual background of geometrization**.

## Thurston / 3-manifold geometry

- **Ken'ichi Ohshika, Athanase Papadopoulos, “A glimpse into Thurston's work.”** arXiv:2009.06344, math.GT / math.HO.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.06344

- **François Laudenbach, Athanase Papadopoulos, “W. P. Thurston and French mathematics.”** arXiv:1912.03115.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.03115

- **Athanase Papadopoulos, “Some footnotes on Thurston's Notes ‘The Geometry and Topology of 3-manifolds’.”** arXiv:2402.08302, math.GT / math.HO.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08302
  Particularly relevant here: hyperbolic geometry, geometric structures, hyperbolic-polyhedron volume, and the Koebe–Andreev–Thurston circle-pattern/polyhedron line.

## Prehistory of hyperbolic geometry

- **Athanase Papadopoulos, “On Lobachevsky's trigonometric formulae.”** arXiv:1311.6034, math.HO.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.6034

- **Athanase Papadopoulos, Guillaume Théret, “Hyperbolic geometry in the work of Johann Heinrich Lambert.”** arXiv:1503.01865.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01865

- **Athanase Papadopoulos, Guillaume Théret, “Johann Heinrich Lambert's memoir ‘Theorie der Parallellinien’: A review with commentary.”** arXiv:2602.08420, math.HO.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08420

The value for `montesinos` is structural: the spherical/Euclidean/hyperbolic split used in tessellation and orbifold work did not arrive as a finished taxonomy. These papers make the emergence of the hyperbolic side visible.

## Spherical side

- **Athanase Papadopoulos, “Spherical trigonometry before the modern era: The treatise of Nasir al-Din al-Tusi.”** arXiv:2605.03516, math.HO.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03516

- **Athanase Papadopoulos, “Euler and Chebyshev: From the sphere to the plane and backwards.”** arXiv:1608.02724, math.HO / math.DG / math.GT.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.02724

These are not 3-manifold papers, but they belong near the spherical/hyperbolic tessellation material and the map-distortion questions that later meet conformal geometry.
